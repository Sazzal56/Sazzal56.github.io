Privacy policy: Snooze Slide

Lamplit Games · Effective 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Snooze Slide mobile game ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. Snooze Slide is made by Lamplit Games, an independent developer ("we").

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your phone.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. These Google services collect device information described below.
  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Privacy choices.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, stars, streaks and settings (sound, vibration) are saved in a file on your phone. We don't receive this file. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information collected by services we use

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (ads) Advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), performance diagnostics such as app launch time, and how you interact with ads To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, and (only with consent where required) personalise ads
Google Firebase Analytics An app instance ID, device and app information, gameplay events such as levels started, won or quit, time played and ads shown, and, if Google Play provides it, which link the game was installed from (for example a link on our TikTok page; no personal details) To see which levels are too hard, fix problems, improve the game, and learn which of our videos bring players
Google Play Crash and performance reports, as set up in your phone's Google settings To find and fix crashes
Game settings file (GitHub Pages) When the game starts, it downloads a small settings file from our website, which is hosted by GitHub. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information. The game sends nothing else To adjust things like how often ads appear, or to show a news line, without an app update

This information is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We only see aggregated reports (for example, how many players finished level 20), not information that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting data: uninstall the game to remove everything stored on your phone. To ask Google about data it holds, use the tools in your Google account.

Children

Snooze Slide is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have a concern, contact us below.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information and we don't share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above, who process it to provide their services.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google is transferred over encrypted connections. Google keeps analytics and advertising data for the periods set in its own policies and our account settings (for Firebase Analytics, data retention is set to the shortest available period).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Sazzal56/Sazzal56.github.io/issues.
