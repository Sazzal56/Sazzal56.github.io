Puzzle of the day: level 170

1 October 2026 · Study · 7 × 7 · best: 9 swipes

Today's puzzle is level 170 of Snooze Slide, from the Study: Ginger the ginger cat, Blue the grey cat and Callie the calico cat on a 7 by 7 floor, with one-way cat flaps, a ball of yarn and a few other things in the way. Every cat has to end up asleep on its own bed, and the best solution takes 9 swipes.

What makes this one tricky

There is exactly one way to do it in 9. Only one of the cats can go to bed first.

New to the game? One swipe moves every cat until something stops it, a cat only falls asleep when it stops on its own bed, and a sleeping cat becomes a wall for the others. The four rules are here.

Hints, one at a time

Hint 1: which cat goes to bed first?

Callie, the calico cat.

Hint 2: the first swipe

Swipe up.

Hint 3: halfway there

After 5 swipes of the best solution, Callie is asleep.

The solution

Show all 9 swipes
  1. ↑ up
  2. ← left · Callie falls asleep
  3. → right
  4. ↓ down
  5. ← left
  6. ↓ down
  7. → right
  8. ↑ up · Blue falls asleep
  9. ← left · Ginger falls asleep
