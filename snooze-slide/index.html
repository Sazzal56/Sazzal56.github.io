Questions

What kind of game is it?

A calm logic puzzle with cats. Each swipe slides every cat in the room at once; get each cat onto its own bed. Once a cat is asleep it stays put and blocks the others, so the order matters. There are soft cushions, cardboard boxes, one-way cat flaps and rolling balls of yarn as you go from room to room.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free to play, with ads. Hints come with an optional short ad; there are never ads at launch or in the middle of a puzzle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every puzzle works offline.

Is there a timer or lives?

No. No timers, no lives, no rush. Undo any swipe, restart anytime.
